How it works

One class period. No preparation.

01

They pick a business

A tea stall, a stationery shop, or a tiffin service. Each one behaves differently — one spoils if it goes unsold, one does not.

02

They set a price and buy stock

Every week, two decisions. Demand answers back. Rain, a festival, a competitor opening next door — none of it is theirs to control.

03

They find out why

The ledger shows exactly where the money went. If they ask why, a tutor explains it in two sentences — and gives one thing to try.

What the student does

  • Runs eight weeks in about three minutes
  • Starts with ₹5,000 and can lose all of it
  • Sees a live break-even figure while choosing a price
  • Earns coins for profit and for judging stock well
  • Cannot refresh to escape a bad week — the weather is fixed

What the teacher does

  • Creates a class and reads out a six-character code
  • Nothing to install, on any device with a browser
  • Sees who is on which week, and who is losing money
  • Sees the class pattern — "18 of 30 priced below cost"
  • Exports the results for their own records

What students learn

Five things, none of them memorised.

Each one is learned by a decision going wrong, not by a definition going into a notebook.

Price is not a guess

Cut the price and more people want it — but you may now need to sell more than exist. Raise it and you need fewer, but fewer want it.

Break-even, felt rather than defined

The number of units needed just to cover the rent updates while they move the price. Nobody has to explain the formula.

Profit is not cash

Stock is paid for before anything sells. A student can post a profit on paper and still run out of money — and does.

Demand is not yours to control

Rain halves the week. A festival doubles it. The lesson is planning for a week you did not choose.

Buying too much is how shops die

Unsold tea is thrown away. Most students go bankrupt by over-buying, once — and then never again.

Failure costs nothing here

Going broke takes thirty seconds and teaches more than a correct answer on a worksheet. They can start again immediately.
